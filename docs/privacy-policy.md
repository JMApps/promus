# Privacy Policy — ПроМус

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

This policy describes how the **ProMus** (Provision of the Muslim) mobile app for iOS and Android handles user information.

## Data collection

**We do not collect, store on servers, or share with third parties any personal data.**

The app requires no account, uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not send data over the internet.

## Data stored on your device

To function, the app stores locally on your device:

- your selected city and its coordinates (chosen manually from a built-in list or added by you) — to calculate prayer times;
- app settings: calculation method, adjustments, theme, colors, notifications;
- tasbeeh counter results and other preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Notifications

The app uses local notifications for prayer time reminders. They are scheduled on the device, without any servers. You can revoke notification permission in system settings.

## Widgets

The home screen widget receives data (prayer times) only from the app itself on your device.

## Sharing

When you share text from the app, it is passed to the app you choose via the standard system share sheet. We have no access to that data.

## Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
